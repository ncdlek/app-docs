# Capture GO Support

Welcome to Capture GO support. If your question is not answered below, write to
**engin@yazilan.org** — please include your device model and iOS version.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What is the goal of the game?**
You win by capturing the opponent's stones. Before a game starts you choose the
goal: **one capture, three, or five.** At one capture the very first stone taken
ends the game, which is what makes it a good way to learn. This is the "capture
game", also called Atari Go — a teaching variant of Go that skips territory
scoring entirely.

**How do I capture a stone?**
Surround it on every side along the lines of the board — not diagonally. When a
stone has no empty point left next to it, it is captured and comes off the
board. Those empty neighbouring points are called *liberties*, and putting a
stone down to one last liberty is called *atari*.

**Do I need an internet connection?**
No. The AI opponent runs entirely on your iPhone, so the whole game works
offline, including in airplane mode. No account is required to play.

**Is online play available?**
Not at the moment. Online multiplayer is switched off: there were too few
players for matchmaking to reliably find you an opponent. Everything else in the
app is unaffected.

**Is the game free?**
Yes. You can play against the AI for free, and life stones refill on their own
over time. There are optional boosts and cosmetic items in the shop, but nothing
in the game is locked behind them.

**When does a life stone get used?**
Only when you *lose* an AI game. Wins, draws and Practice games cost nothing,
and your first few games are free while the difficulty settles to your level.
Stones refill automatically.

**How do I collect Shapes?**
Shapes are patterns formed by stones on the board. When a recognised shape
appears during one of your games it is added to your Collection album
automatically.

**Can I use the app with VoiceOver?**
Yes. Every control has a VoiceOver label, the app supports Dynamic Type through
the accessibility text sizes, and it respects Reduce Motion.

**Which languages does it support?**
English, German, French, Japanese, Korean, Russian, Turkish and Simplified
Chinese.

**I bought coins or a pass and did not receive it.**
Purchases are restored from your Apple ID. Try Shop → Restore Purchases first.
If that does not work, email **engin@yazilan.org** with the date of purchase and
we will sort it out.

**How do I delete my data?**
Email **engin@yazilan.org** from the address linked to your account, or see the
[Privacy Policy](privacypolicy.html).
