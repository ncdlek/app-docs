faq:
  - q: What is the goal of the game?
    a: >-
      You win by capturing the opponent's stones. Before a game starts you
      choose the goal: **one capture, three, or five.** At one capture the very
      first stone taken ends the game, which is what makes it a good way to
      learn. This is the "capture game", also called Atari Go — a teaching
      variant of Go that skips territory scoring entirely.
  - q: How do I capture a stone?
    a: >-
      Surround it on every side along the lines of the board — not
      diagonally. When a stone has no empty point left next to it, it is
      captured and comes off the board. Those empty neighbouring points are
      called *liberties*, and putting a stone down to one last liberty is
      called *atari*.
  - q: Do I need an internet connection?
    a: >-
      Not to play the AI. It runs entirely on your iPhone, so games against it
      work offline, including in airplane mode, and no account is required.
      Online matches need a connection.
  - q: How does online play work?
    a: |-
      Before a game starts, tap the Quick Match button beside the board. If
      another player is waiting you are matched straight away; otherwise the
      app waits up to 30 seconds for someone to join. The server draws each
      online game's opening (the classic four-stone start or an empty board)
      and its capture goal — one, three or five — and the reels on the board
      show you what came up.

      Every move has a 60-second clock. If it runs out, you lose the game, and
      leaving a game before it ends counts as a loss too. Online play needs
      the latest version of the app.
  - q: Is the game free?
    a: >-
      Yes. You can play against the AI for free, and life stones refill on
      their own over time. There are optional boosts and cosmetic items in the
      shop, but nothing in the game is locked behind them.
  - q: When does a life stone get used?
    a: >-
      Only when you *lose* an AI game. Wins, draws and Practice games cost
      nothing, and your first few games are free while the difficulty settles
      to your level. Stones refill automatically.
  - q: How do I collect Shapes?
    a: >-
      Shapes are patterns formed by stones on the board. When a recognised
      shape appears during one of your games it is added to your Collection
      album automatically.
  - q: Can I use the app with VoiceOver?
    a: >-
      Yes. Every control has a VoiceOver label, the app supports Dynamic Type
      through the accessibility text sizes, and it respects Reduce Motion.
  - q: Which languages does it support?
    a: >-
      Arabic, Chinese (Simplified and Traditional), Czech, Dutch, English,
      French, German, Indonesian, Italian, Japanese, Korean, Polish,
      Portuguese (Brazil and Portugal), Russian, Spanish, Thai, Turkish,
      Ukrainian and Vietnamese.
  - q: I bought coins or a pass and did not receive it.
    a: >-
      Purchases are restored from your Apple ID. Try Shop → Restore Purchases
      first. If that does not work, email **engin@yazilan.org** with the date
      of purchase and we will sort it out.
  - q: How do I delete my data?
    a: >-
      Email **engin@yazilan.org** from the address linked to your account, or
      see the [Privacy Policy](privacypolicy.html).
