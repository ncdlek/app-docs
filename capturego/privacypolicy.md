# Privacy Policy for Capture GO

**Effective Date:** September 6, 2026
**Previous version:** January 13, 2026

Capture GO ("we", "us", "our") values your privacy. This policy describes what
the app collects, why, and what it does not do.

## 1. The short version

- The AI opponent runs **entirely on your device**. Your moves are not sent
  anywhere to generate them.
- **No advertising, and no tracking across other apps or websites.** The app
  contains no advertising SDKs and never asks for tracking permission, because
  it never tracks you.
- **No account is required to play.**
- We do not sell your personal data.

## 2. What we collect

**Anonymous account identifier.** When you first open the app an anonymous
account is created so your progress — coins, streaks, collected shapes,
unlocked proverbs — survives reinstalling. It is not linked to your name or
email.

**Sign in with Apple (optional).** If you choose to sign in, we store the
identifier Apple gives us so your progress can follow you to another device. If
you use Apple's Hide My Email, we only ever see the relay address.

**Profile you choose.** A username and an avatar emoji, which are yours to
change, plus your total play time and the date your account was created.

**Gameplay data.** Games you finish are stored so the app can show your history
and replay them move by move. Local games are stored **on your device**; if you
are signed in, finished games may also be archived to our servers so they are
not lost when you change device.

**Purchases.** If you buy anything, Apple handles the payment and we never see
your card or billing details. We receive the fact of the purchase and its
status, through RevenueCat, so the app knows what you own.

**Crash reports and usage analytics.** We collect crash diagnostics and
aggregated, non-identifying usage events (for example, that a tutorial step was
completed) to fix bugs and understand which parts of the app are used. These are
tied to the anonymous identifier above, not to you personally.

**Game Center.** If you use Game Center, leaderboards and achievements are
handled by Apple under Apple's own privacy policy. Your Game Center identity is
not shared with us beyond what Apple provides for verifying it.

## 3. Who processes data for us

- **Google Firebase** — accounts, database, file storage, crash reporting,
  analytics, and abuse prevention.
- **RevenueCat** — subscription and purchase status.
- **Apple** — payments, Sign in with Apple, and Game Center.

These providers process data on our behalf. We do not sell data to anyone.

## 4. Online multiplayer

Online multiplayer is **currently switched off** in the app, so no match data is
being exchanged with other players. If it returns, this policy will be updated
before it does, and the section below will describe it accurately:

> When online play is active, a username and avatar emoji you have chosen are
> visible to your opponent, and moves are relayed through our servers to keep
> both sides of the game in sync.

## 5. Children

Capture GO is rated 4+ and is suitable for children. We do not knowingly collect
personal information from children, and the app does not require an account,
does not contain advertising, and does not ask for a name, email address or
location.

## 6. Data security and retention

Connections use industry-standard encryption. Game archives and profile data are
kept while your account exists. You can ask us to delete everything associated
with your account at any time.

## 7. Your choices

- Play without signing in, and nothing is linked to an Apple ID.
- Delete the app to remove all locally stored games.
- Ask us to delete your server-side data — see below.

## 8. Contact

For privacy questions or a data deletion request, contact **engin@yazilan.org**.
Please write from the address linked to your account, or tell us your in-app
username, so we can find the right records.
