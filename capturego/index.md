faq:
  - q: What is Capture GO?
    a: >-
      Capture GO is a free iPhone and iPad game that teaches Go (Weiqi, Baduk)
      through the capture variant, Atari Go. The first player to capture a
      stone wins, so a game takes minutes and the only rule you need is how
      to surround a stone.
  - q: What is Atari Go, and how is it different from regular Go?
    a: >-
      Atari Go is the "capture game" Go teachers use with beginners. The
      board, stones and capturing rule are the same as in Go, but instead of
      counting territory at the end, the game ends as soon as someone
      captures. Capture GO plays it on a 9×9 board and lets you raise the goal
      to three or five captures.
  - q: Is Capture GO a good way for a complete beginner or a child to learn Go?
    a: >-
      Yes. It is rated 4+, has no ads, and the tutorial defines every term —
      liberty, atari, capture — before it uses it. New players start against
      the beginner-level AI.
  - q: Does Capture GO work offline?
    a: >-
      Yes. The AI opponent runs entirely on your iPhone or iPad, so games
      against it work in airplane mode and need no account. Only online
      matches need a connection.
  - q: Is Capture GO free?
    a: >-
      Yes. The whole game is playable for free. Optional boosts and cosmetic
      items are sold in the shop; nothing is pay-to-win.
  - q: Is there an Android version?
    a: No. Capture GO is available only on the App Store, for iPhone and iPad.
