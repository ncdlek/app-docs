Game Clock Pro ("we", "us", "our") is a game clock. It does not need to know
anything about you, so it does not ask.

## 1. The short version

- **We collect no data.** There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no
  crash-reporting service and no advertising.
- **The app never connects to the internet.** It contains no third-party SDKs
  and makes no network requests.
- **Everything stays on your device.** Your settings, theme and saved time
  presets are stored locally and are removed when you delete the app.

## 2. What is stored on your device

The time controls, alerts, theme and presets you choose are kept in the app's
local storage on your iPhone or iPad so they are there the next time you open
it. They are not sent to us or to anyone else.

## 3. Things Apple handles

- **Purchase.** Game Clock Pro is bought through the App Store. Apple
  processes the payment; we never see your name, Apple ID or payment details.
- **Ratings.** If you tap "Rate on App Store", the rating prompt is shown and
  handled by Apple.
- **Crash reports.** If you have chosen to share analytics with app developers
  in your device settings, Apple may pass us anonymous crash reports. You can
  turn this off in Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements.

## 4. Contacting us

If you use "Contact Developer", your mail app sends an ordinary email. We use
your address and message only to reply, and we delete them on request.

## 5. Children

Game Clock Pro is rated 4+. Because it collects no personal information from
anyone, it collects none from children.

## 6. Changes to this policy

If the app ever starts handling data, this page will be updated before that
version is released, and the date at the top will change.
