faq:
  - q: How do I start a game and pass the turn?
    a: >-
      Tap the play button to start. Black's clock is on top and White's on
      the bottom. After you move, tap your own side of the screen: your
      clock stops and your opponent's starts.
  - q: How do I pause or reset?
    a: >-
      Tap the pause button between the two clocks. While the game is paused,
      the reset button returns both clocks to their starting time; the app
      asks you to confirm first.
  - q: Where do I change the time control?
    a: >-
      Open Settings with the gear icon (it is available when no game is
      running) and choose a time control: Japanese byo-yomi, Canadian
      byo-yomi, Fischer, Bronstein, delay or absolute. The fields below it
      change to match.
  - q: How do Japanese byo-yomi periods work?
    a: >-
      When your main time runs out you enter byo-yomi. Each period has a
      fixed length — say 30 seconds. Move before it runs out and the period
      starts again in full; let it run out and you lose one period. When the
      last period runs out, your time is up.
  - q: How does Canadian byo-yomi work?
    a: >-
      After main time you get a block of overtime — for example 5 minutes —
      in which you must play a set number of stones or moves. Reach the
      number in time and a fresh block starts.
  - q: What is the difference between Fischer, Bronstein and delay?
    a: >-
      **Fischer** adds a fixed increment after every move, so your time can
      grow (up to an optional maximum). **Bronstein** gives back the time you
      used on a move, but never more than the delay, so your time never
      grows. **Delay** waits for a few seconds each turn before your main time
      starts to count down.
  - q: How do I give a handicap?
    a: >-
      In Settings, turn on Time Handicap and choose how many minutes to add.
      They are added to Black's main time, which in Go is the weaker
      player's side.
  - q: How do I save my own time settings?
    a: >-
      Set up the time control you want, then save it under Saved Times in
      Settings. Use "Manage saved times" to edit or delete presets, or to
      restore the built-in Blitz, Coffee and Tournament presets.
  - q: Can the clock count down out loud?
    a: >-
      Yes. Under Alerts choose Countdown to hear the last seconds read out,
      Beep for a tone, or None for silence. You can set when the alert starts,
      add vibration, and have the last byo-yomi period announced.
  - q: Will my screen turn off during a game?
    a: >-
      No. The screen stays on while a game is running. When the game is
      paused or over, the normal auto-lock comes back and the app lowers its
      refresh rate to save battery.
  - q: Does it work on iPad?
    a: >-
      Yes. On iPad it also works in landscape. On iPhone it runs in portrait,
      with the top clock turned toward the player across the table.
  - q: I changed phones. Do I need to buy it again?
    a: >-
      No. Open the App Store, tap your profile picture, then Purchased, and
      download Game Clock Pro again with the same Apple ID. Saved presets are
      stored on the old device and do not move across.
  - q: Which languages does it support?
    a: >-
      Arabic, Chinese (Simplified), Croatian, Dutch, English, French, German,
      Greek, Italian, Japanese, Korean, Portuguese, Romanian, Russian, Spanish,
      Turkish and Ukrainian.
